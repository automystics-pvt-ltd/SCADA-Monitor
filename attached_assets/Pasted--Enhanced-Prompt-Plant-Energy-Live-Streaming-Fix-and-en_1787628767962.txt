### Enhanced Prompt — Plant Energy Live Streaming

Fix and enable the **Plant Energy Live Streaming** functionality as a critical part of the Solar SCADA application. The energy stream must operate continuously using the **actual live MQTT/Modbus telemetry received from the connected devices**.

The visualization should clearly show the **real-time flow of solar energy generation** so users can immediately understand that the device is communicating correctly and that live data is being received.

* Stream the **actual current plant/inverter power data** into the energy visualization.
* Update the visualization automatically whenever new valid telemetry arrives.
* Use the actual device values—**never simulated, hard-coded, estimated, or fabricated data**.
* Clearly represent the flow such as:
  **Solar Plant → Inverter → AC Power → Plant Output/Grid**, based only on parameters actually available from the device.
* Show the **current Actual Value, unit, timestamp, and Live status** alongside the animation where appropriate.
* If multiple inverters are available, dynamically reflect their contribution to the overall plant energy/power flow.
* The animation should visibly respond to actual generation levels rather than running as a decorative animation independent of telemetry.
* If generation is zero, the animation should accurately represent **zero/no generation** rather than pretending energy is flowing.
* If live telemetry temporarily stops, retain the **latest valid saved record** and clearly indicate that the visualization is showing saved data rather than falsely presenting it as live.
* Resume live streaming automatically when valid telemetry returns.
* Ensure the energy stream does not stop because of unrelated MQTT/frontend connection-state issues when valid telemetry is still being received.
* Avoid unnecessary frontend re-renders that could cause lag or interrupt the streaming visualization.

### Critical SCADA Requirement

The flow visualization must be driven by the actual telemetry pipeline:

**Device → MQTT → Backend → Validate → Actual Power Value → Plant Energy Stream → Dashboard**

It should provide a clear visual confirmation that:

**Device is communicating → Data is arriving → Data is valid → Plant generation is being reflected**

The Plant Energy Stream must remain **smooth, responsive, accurate, lightweight, and continuously synchronized with real SCADA telemetry**, without affecting existing MQTT/Modbus communication, data-saving logic, dashboard functionality, or other application features.

**Do not use decorative animation disconnected from the actual data. The animation itself must represent genuine SCADA plant generation data.**
